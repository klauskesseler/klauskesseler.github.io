*Datenschutzerklärung*

This notice explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website, and why.

## Who is responsible

Klaus Kesseler, contact details as in the [Impressum](/impressum/).

## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data, such as the time, the page requested and your browser, to deliver the website and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable website. This data may be processed in the USA. Details, including the safeguards GitHub uses for international transfers, are in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Visitor statistics

I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to see how many people read which pages. It records the page visited, the referring page, an approximate country derived from your IP address, your browser and operating system, your preferred language and your screen width. It does not store IP addresses and sets no cookies or other identifiers in your browser. To avoid counting the same visit twice, it keeps a temporary, randomly generated identifier in memory for up to eight hours. The statistics are stored in aggregated form on servers in Finland and Germany and are not shared with third parties.

GoatCounter is operated by Martin Tournoij (Ireland). To load its counting script, your browser connects to GoatCounter's server, which receives your IP address for that purpose. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in understanding which content is useful, without tracking individuals. More in the [GoatCounter privacy documentation](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

## Cookies

This website sets no cookies.

## Contacting me

If you contact me by email, I process your message and contact details only to reply, on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16), erased (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW).

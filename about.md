I'm Klaus Kesseler, a software architect and engineering leader based in Germany.

Over twenty years I have built systems ranging from GPU rendering pipelines and 3D content delivery to SaaS platforms for subscriptions, payments and identity used by Tier 1 telecom operators. I have built and led engineering teams across North America, Europe and India.

Today my focus is agentic AI in production: agent harnesses, context and knowledge-graph layers, and the security and evaluation work that lets agents do real delivery work.

I studied computer science at RWTH Aachen, with a focus on computer graphics and artificial intelligence.

Everything on this site is my own view and does not represent any employer.

I'm Klaus Kesseler, a software architect and engineering leader based near Düsseldorf.

## What I work on

For the past two years, agentic AI that does real delivery work. I designed and built an agent platform with its context and knowledge-graph layer, and the parts that decide whether anyone trusts it: role-based permissions, sandboxed execution, a full audit trail, graph-defined workflows, human approval gates, and eval gates before anything ships. The models change every few months; the engineering around them is what lasts.

## Before that

Twenty years of building systems that had to run at scale: GPU and CUDA rendering for 3D displays, a 3D content delivery network, and identity, subscription and payment platforms for Tier 1 telecom operators with up to 40 million users. Along the way I moved from Germany to Canada and back, grew an engineering organisation from 20 to 120 people through an acquisition, and built teams across North America, Europe and India. I co-authored several image-processing patents and patent applications.

## About this site

Notes on what survived production. Each post makes one claim, shows the arithmetic, states the caveat and ends with what I'd do. If the numbers don't hold up, I'd rather hear it.
